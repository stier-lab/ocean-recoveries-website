# Kelp forest habitat pencil study

AI-assisted illustration for Resilience Across Scales. The habitat reference is [Robert Schwemmer's NOAA photograph of a Channel Islands kelp forest](https://channelislands.noaa.gov/media/img/sheephead-kelp-forest.jpg). The paired `urchin-barren-pencil-v2.png` guided the pencil texture and low reef viewpoint. The drawing illustrates a habitat, not a survey site or a measured comparison.
