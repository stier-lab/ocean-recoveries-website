## Quantitative Ecology: Reef-to-Model Artwork Selected

Adrian selected the standalone reef-becoming-a-model study, requested less perfect handwritten math, and authorized adding it to the local Quantitative Ecology card for mobile review before pushing. Current artwork: `how-we-work-quantitative-ecology-reef-model-pencil.png`, 1536 x 1024, with a 1200 x 800 quality-88 JPEG under `public/images/research/`. Reference sequence: initial standalone study `exec-85266d2c-867c-4595-9d6f-d042e7faa380.png`; equation-bearing study `exec-7b231fd2-3529-4eee-afd1-c50820e4322b.png`; final handwriting edit `exec-d73decee-5aaf-4c5e-bf76-2ef0b39e26b8.png`, all in the generated-images folder below. Initial references were `pocillopora-with-coral-associates-v3.png` and the prior Quantitative Ecology scatterplot for pencil treatment. The final edit specifically requested variable graphite pressure, irregular letter sizes/slants, and lightly retraced fraction bars while preserving the equation and biological composition.

The equation is `dC/dt = rC(1 - C/K) + aCF/(h + F)`: density-limited growth of coral biomass C plus a saturating benefit from resident fish abundance F. r is baseline growth rate, K baseline carrying capacity, a the maximum per-biomass fish contribution, and h the half-saturation fish abundance. This is an illustrative focal-coral equation, not a fitted result, a full coupled mutualism model, or an equation attributed to a lab paper. The reciprocal arrows are conceptual; only the coral equation is displayed. Visually checked the signs, numerator aCF, denominator h+F, and parentheses after editing.
