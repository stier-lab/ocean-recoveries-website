# Kelp forest colored-pencil studies

These AI-assisted drawings match the medium of the Mo'orea Pocillopora study. The giant kelp plate emphasizes the floats, blades, and holdfast. It is not a survey record or a scale drawing.

The original PNGs and their anatomy references:

- `california-spiny-lobster-pencil.png`: `public/images/spiny-lobsters-group-reef-hideout.jpeg`
- `purple-sea-urchin-pencil.png`: `public/images/purple-urchin.jpeg`
- `california-sheephead-pencil.png`: `public/images/california-sheephead-kelp-forest.jpeg`

The style reference was `pocillopora-with-coral-associates-v3.png`. The Kelp Forests page uses compressed JPEGs from `public/images/research/`.
